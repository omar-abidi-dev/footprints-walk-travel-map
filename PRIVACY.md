# Footprints: Walk & Travel Map — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

Footprints is made by Omar Abidi ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Footprints iPhone app.

## The short version

- Footprints has **no account and no sign-up**. I don't run any server and I never receive your walks, places, photos or location.
- Everything you create in the app is **stored on your iPhone**.
- The app is **free and shows ads from Google AdMob**. Google may collect some device and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.

## Information stored on your iPhone

- **Walks**: the GPS route, distance, time, pace, elevation and any title or photo you add.
- **Places**: name, location, category, note, photo, favorite status and visit count.
- **Imported Google Timeline data**: if you choose to import a Google Timeline export, the file is read on your iPhone and turned into places and walks. It is not uploaded anywhere.
- **Settings and progress**: badges, achievements, level and preferences.

This data stays on your device. It is only copied elsewhere if you choose to make a backup to the Files app or iCloud Drive, and then it goes only to the location you pick. Deleting the app deletes this data from your iPhone.

## Location

Footprints asks for location access **While Using the App** so it can draw your walks and pin places. While you are actively recording a walk, the app can keep receiving location updates when your iPhone is locked, so the route keeps recording. Location is never sent to me.

## Services the app connects to

To show maps and find places, the app uses these services, which receive standard request information such as your IP address and the map area or search being requested:

- **Apple Maps / MapKit**: maps, place search and turning coordinates into place names. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **OpenStreetMap** map tiles (tile.openstreetmap.org), if you choose the street map style. See the [OSMF Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
- **Esri ArcGIS** satellite imagery (server.arcgisonline.com), if you choose the satellite style. See [Esri's Privacy Statement](https://www.esri.com/en-us/privacy/overview).

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

Footprints shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

- Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) **only if you allow tracking** when iOS asks
- Approximate location derived from your IP address
- Information about the ads you see and tap (advertising data and product interaction)
- Diagnostic and performance information (for example crash and timing data)

When the app first runs, you may be asked for consent through Google's consent message (where required by law, such as in the EEA and UK) and through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change your choice anytime in **You → Settings → Privacy choices** in the app, and in iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**. If you don't allow tracking, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.

Learn more in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Children

Footprints is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
