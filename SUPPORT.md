## Frequently asked questions

**My walk stopped recording when I locked my phone.**
Make sure location access for Footprints is set to *While Using the App* or *Always* in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Footprints, and that Low Power Mode isn't limiting background activity.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your iPhone. There's no account. To keep a copy, use **You → Settings → Back up to Files or iCloud Drive**, and **Restore from backup** on a new phone.

**How do I import my Google Timeline?**
Export your Timeline from the Google Maps app on your phone (Settings → Location & privacy → Export Timeline data), then in Footprints go to **You → Settings → Import Google Timeline** and choose the file.

**Why doesn't a visit count toward a badge?**
Badges update after each footprint has been matched to a region. If you see "Placing your footprints" on the Badges tab, wait a moment while it finishes, or use **You → Settings → Retry locating regions**.

**How do I change my ad privacy choices?**
Go to **You → Settings → Privacy choices**, or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

**How do I delete my data?**
**You → Settings → Erase all footprints** removes every place and walk. Deleting the app also removes all its data from your iPhone.
